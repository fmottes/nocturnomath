Nocturnomath

Nocturnomaths are rare, many-brained life forms found chiefly in academies, laboratories, and other places where an unanswered question is likely to occur. Their rank is determined by the number of brains they possess. This is a measure of intellectual capacity, not infallibility, although few Nocturnomaths consider the distinction worth making.

Their brains can pursue several lines of thought at once, which makes a Nocturnomath extremely difficult to outreason and almost impossible to interrupt. They cultivate intelligence bacteria capable of transferring large bodies of knowledge directly into another creature. The procedure is rapid and effective, though recipients may afterwards suffer an urge to correct encyclopaedias.

The best-known member of the species is Professor Abdullah Nightingale, the seven-brained director of the Nocturnal Academy in the Gloomberg Mountains. Dr Oztafan Kolibril, who possesses four brains, is chiefly remembered for investigations into the very small and the inadvisably experimental. Travellers encountering either are advised to carry a notebook, allow several hours, and avoid beginning any sentence with the words “Surely it is obvious that…”
